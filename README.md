# Cybersecurity Portfolio

## About Me

I'm a cyber threat intelligence analyst with more than a decade of experience in
intelligence and security analysis. I began my career as an analyst working with
remote sensing to analyze signals and geospatial data, then moved into cybersecurity 
to track threats across networks. Today, I investigate intrusions, analyze malware, 
and profile advanced persistent threat (APT) groups, turning technical findings into 
actionable intelligence that supports security operations and helps leadership and 
security teams reduce risk. 

What continues to draw me to cybersecurity is the same core interest that drew me
to intelligence: taking complex, incomplete information, applying structured
methods, and turning it into actionable insight, now directed at protecting
critical systems and understanding sophisticated digital threats.

### Highlights

- **Cyber threat intelligence:** threat actor profiling, TTP analysis, and threat
  attribution using MITRE ATT&CK, the Diamond Model, and the Cyber Kill Chain
- **Detection and analysis:** log analysis, network traffic analysis, and IOC
  enrichment to connect indicators to attacker behavior
- **Automation:** Python and Jupyter notebooks for detection engineering and
  reporting; SQL for data engineering
- **OSINT and attack surface analysis:** infrastructure mapping, behavioral
  baselining, and digital footprint analysis
